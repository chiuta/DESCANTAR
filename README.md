## Features

- Three methods: “by the book” 3×3 (default), the Oltenian variant with its special signs, and a simple three-pile method.
- Reading by head / heart / feet, odd–even signs, a YES/NO tally and a copyable result; history of past readings.
- 12 traditional charms, moon phase, recitation counter, charm generator and a copyable guide.

## Usage

Open the page online, or download `index.html` and open it in a recent browser. No installation, no build.

## Limits

- Entertainment and folklore, not advice; do not use it for medical, legal or financial decisions.
- The Oltenian rules were taken from a text I could not verify; one line of the traditional charm is unclear and is marked as a local variant.
- Interface and folklore content (readings, charms, guide) in 8 languages; the translations of the traditional texts were AI-generated and not reviewed by native speakers.

## Privacy

No tracking, no network requests (CSP `connect-src none`), no accounts. Data stays in this browser (localStorage) and can be erased at any time.
